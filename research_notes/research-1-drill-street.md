## Q1: Chicago drill (2012–2014) — release/premiere channel, velocity data, overnight-fame evidence

### Takeaway
Chicago drill's ignition was measurable almost entirely in **YouTube views on director-owned channels** (DGainz above all), not on label or WSHH channels — the scene's own view-count leaderboard *was* the metric of stardom. "I Don't Like" is the canonical 5/5: 30M+ views on a teenager's videographer's channel inside ~17 months, a Kanye remix 7 weeks after the video, and a $6M Interscope deal inside 6 months. Hard first-week numbers for individual drill videos are essentially unrecoverable without archive access.

### Cited Findings

**Chief Keef — "I Don't Like" (ft. Lil Reese), 2012 — IGNITION 5 (benchmark)**
- Song released **March 6, 2012**; DGainz-directed video uploaded to **DGainz's own YouTube channel March 11, 2012** — HARD — [Wikipedia: I Don't Like](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Don%27t_Like) (accessed 2026-09-19, via search summary)
- The DGainz upload **amassed over 30 million views before it was removed in August 2013**; the Vevo re-upload passed **100M views as of March 2018** — HARD (cumulative, not first-week) — [Wikipedia: I Don't Like](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Don%27t_Like)
- **Kanye West's "I Don't Like" remix premiered Tuesday, May 1, 2012** (Pusha T, Big Sean, Jadakiss) — ~7 weeks after the video upload — HARD — [Stereogum, May 2012](https://stereogum.com/1019512/kanye-west-i-dont-like-remix-feat-chief-keef-pusha-t-jadakiss-big-sean/news); [Fake Shore Drive, May 2012](https://www.fakeshoredrive.com/2012/05/chief-keef-f-kanye-west-pusha-t-jadakiss-big-sean-i-dont-like-remix.html/)
- "The song came by way of WorldStarHipHop, and by spring of 2012 the music video had millions of views… young hip-hop consumers were more apt to run to WorldStarHipHop than 106 & Park" — VIBE — [Complex, 10-year anniversary piece](https://www.complex.com/music/a/andre-gee/chief-keef-i-dont-like-10-year-anniversary)
- Scene-metric quote: per A Zae Production, "rap stardom in the windy city was defined by YouTube views in 2012 — whoever popped up with the most views at any given moment was 'the man'" — VIBE but structurally important — [Complex](https://www.complex.com/music/a/andre-gee/chief-keef-i-dont-like-10-year-anniversary)
- **Unknown-to-famous proof:** bidding war involving Birdman, T.I. and Jeezy/CTE World; Keef signed a **three-year, three-album Interscope deal worth $6,000,000, plus a $440,000 advance to establish GBE** — HARD — [HipHopDX](https://hiphopdx.com/news/chief-keefs-deal-with-interscope-records-revealed-to-be-worth-6-million/); [HotNewHipHop](https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/455585-details-of-chief-keef-s-interscope-deal-emerge-worth-s6-million-news)
- **Spark:** A 16-year-old on house arrest turned a videographer's YouTube channel into a national A&R feed in under eight weeks.
- **Billboard footnote:** "I Don't Like" charted modestly; the Kanye remix landed on *Cruel Summer*. Chart position is irrelevant to this song's detonation.

- A contemporaneous (late-2012) source cited **"over 20 million views on YouTube"** as evidence Keef was no one-hit wonder — i.e. ~20M inside roughly 2 months — **HARD but imprecisely dated** (the search summary did not pin the exact article date; treat as approximate) — surfaced via [Complex timeline](https://complex.com/music/2012/12/chief-keef-timeline/chief-keef-releases-love-sosa) / [Wikipedia: Love Sosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Sosa)

### Inferences
- The ignition metric that actually mattered in Chicago 2012 was a **director's YouTube channel**, not WSHH and not DatPiff. DGainz's channel functioned as the scene's chart. Any "modern rap instant proof" playlist that uses 2012 drill should treat DGainz uploads as the premiere channel of record.
- **Major-artist-remix latency** is a usable proxy for ignition velocity where view data is lost: I Don't Like → Kanye in 7 weeks (5/5); Us → Drake/Ross in ~2 months; Traffic → Jeezy/Twista; Kill Shit → Drake public praise.

## Q2: Brooklyn / NYC street wave 2014 (GS9) — Vine detonation numbers

### Takeaway
"Hot Nigga" is the cleanest documented **platform-mismatch detonation** in the era: the video sat on YouTube for ~3 months doing nothing, then one Vine user's 6-second loop of the Shmoney Dance in June 2014 detonated it — 5M YouTube views in a single month, a rumored $2M Epic deal, #1 Hot R&B/Hip-Hop. The lag between upload and ignition is itself the hard evidence.

### Cited Findings

**Bobby Shmurda — "Hot Nigga", 2014 — IGNITION 5 (benchmark)**
- Video **originally uploaded March 28, 2014** (artist-owned YouTube) — HARD — [Wikipedia: Hot Nigga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_Nigga)
- **The video sat until June 2014**, when **Vine user Vladimir Francois looped the Shmoney Dance** — that is the documented ignition point — HARD, named individual — [Billboard: How Bobby Shmurda's "Hot Boy" Came to Life](https://www.billboard.com/pro/how-bobby-shmurda-hot-boy-came-to-life/)
- **Video soared to five million YouTube views in a single month** after ignition — HARD (the single best velocity number available for this record) — [Wikipedia: Hot Nigga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_Nigga)
- Shmoney Dance became a mass meme and was **performed on Beyoncé and Jay Z's On the Run tour** — dated July 15, 2014 coverage, i.e. ~4–6 weeks after ignition — HARD timeline anchor — [Slate, July 15, 2014](https://www.slate.com/blogs/browbeat/2014/07/15/shmoney_dance_bobby_shmurda_viral_hit_goes_from_vines_to_beyonce_and_jay.html)
- **Unknown-to-famous proof:** signed to **Epic Records in 2014 by L.A. Reid in a rumored $2 million deal**, on the heels of the track hitting **#1 on Billboard's Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs** — HARD — [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/bobby-shmurda-epic-records-label-released/); [Variety, 2022](https://variety.com/2022/music/news/bobby-shmurda-released-from-epic-records-deal-1235215848/)
- **Spark:** A Flatbush teenager's hat toss became a six-second Vine loop, and the loop dragged a three-month-old YouTube video to five million views in thirty days.
- **Billboard footnote:** Hot 100 peak **#6** — secondary to the Vine detonation.

**Rowdy Rebel ft. Bobby Shmurda — "Computers", 2014 — IGNITION 3**
- **SOURCE CONFLICT:** FADER dates the **video to August 11, 2014** — [FADER, Aug 11, 2014](https://www.thefader.com/2014/08/11/gs9s-rowdy-rebel-bobby-shmurda-drop-computers-video); a separate summary gives the **song release as October 24, 2014** — [Wikipedia: Rowdy Rebel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rowdy_Rebel). Most likely the video premiered Aug 11 and the commercial single followed in October, but I could not verify. Flag this to the synthesizer.
- Positioned as "the follow-up from Brooklyn's GS9 after their mega-viral 'Hot Nigga'"; markedly higher-budget, whole-crew video — VIBE — [FADER](https://www.thefader.com/2014/08/11/gs9s-rowdy-rebel-bobby-shmurda-drop-computers-video); [Rap Radar, Aug 11, 2014](https://rapradar.com/2014/08/11/new-video-rowdy-rebel-x-bobby-shmurda-computers/)
- **Spark:** GS9's victory lap — proof the 2014 NYC moment was a crew detonation, not one song.
- **Billboard footnote:** no meaningful chart entry found.

### Inferences
- The "Hot Nigga" timeline (upload March 28 → dormant → Vine ignition June → 5M views in a month → #1 R&B/Hip-Hop → $2M deal) is the single best-documented **ignition-velocity case study** in this entire lane, and should probably anchor the synthesizer's calibration for what a 5 looks like when the platform is short-form video rather than streaming.

### Gaps
- **No Vine loop counts** for the Francois clip or any other Shmoney Dance Vine. Vine is dead and its data is not reachable from this environment.
- No WSHH premiere count for "Hot Nigga."
- No first-week Spotify/Apple stream figures.
- No r/hiphopheads thread data.

## Q3: Atlanta trap explosions 2013–2017

### Takeaway
ATL's detonations split into two mechanisms: **co-sign velocity** (Versace→Drake, Tuesday→Drake, Panda→Kanye) and **meme velocity** (U Guessed It on Vine, Bad and Boujee on Twitter, Mask Off as an instrument challenge). Panda has by far the best hard streaming data; Versace and Tuesday have the cleanest co-sign timelines; Bad and Boujee has a precise, quantified meme-to-#1 jump.

### Cited Findings

**Desiigner — "Panda", 2015–16 — IGNITION 5 (benchmark)**
- Released on **SoundCloud December 15, 2015** by an unknown 18-year-old Brooklyn rapper — HARD — [Wikipedia: Panda (song)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panda_(song))
- **Kanye West sampled it for "Father Stretch My Hands Pt. 2"** on *The Life of Pablo* (Feb 2016) — the detonation trigger — HARD — [WhoSampled](https://www.whosampled.com/sample/408802/Kanye-West-Desiigner-Caroline-Shaw-Father-Stretch-My-Hands-Pt.-2-Desiigner-Panda/); [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/desiigner-kanye-west-panda-video-2016/)
- **22.3 million US weekly streams (up 48% week-over-week)**, vaulting **14→5 on the Hot 100** and taking the **Streaming Gainer** award — HARD — [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/desiigner-panda-hot-rap-songs-chart/)
- **#1 on Streaming Songs April 30, 2016 with 28.5 million weekly streams**, rising to **38.5 million weekly streams by May 28, 2016** — HARD — [Wikipedia: List of Billboard Streaming Songs number ones of 2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Billboard_Streaming_Songs_number_ones_of_2016)
- **#1 Hot 100 on May 7, 2016**, ending Rihanna "Work"'s nine-week reign; **first rapper to top the Hot 100 with a debut chart entry since Iggy Azalea's "Fancy"** — HARD — [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/desiigner-panda-billboard-hot-100-number-1/); [FADER, Apr 25, 2016](https://www.thefader.com/2016/04/25/desiigner-panda-no-1-hot-100); [XXL, Apr 2016](https://xxlmag.com/news/2016/04/desiigner-panda-number-one-billboard-hot-chart)
- **Spark:** A $200 SoundCloud beat-lease record by a nobody went from zero to the #1 song in America in under five months because Kanye sampled it.
- **Billboard footnote:** #1 Hot 100. NOT a pop crossover — the first wave was SoundCloud/hip-hop, and the crossover was the *consequence*, not the cause.

**Migos — "Versace", 2013 — IGNITION 4**
- Released **July 2013** via Quality Control on the *Y.R.N.* mixtape — HARD — [Wikipedia: Versace (song)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versace_(song))
- **Co-sign latency, documented:** Drake ran into Migos at Birthday Bash in Atlanta right after *Y.R.N.* dropped and told manager Coach K "Man, I've been listening for the last four days!" — **a week later** Drake reached out to jump on a record — HARD narrative with a stated one-week gap — [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/drake-migos-versace-remix-877645/); [Hypebeast, July 2013](https://hypebeast.com/2013/7/migos-speak-on-drakes-versace-remix)
- "Migos' 'Versace' was buzzing across the country, and Drake added fuel to the hype" — VIBE — [Hypebeast, July 2013](https://hypebeast.com/2013/7/migos-speak-on-drakes-versace-remix)
- Flow-adoption evidence: "when Drake adopted that triplet flow… the whole rap culture took that cadence" — VIBE but the most consequential cultural claim in this lane — [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/drake-migos-versace-remix-877645/)
- Ranked by Billboard among the **100 songs that defined the 2010s** — HARD-ish editorial — [Wikipedia: Versace (song)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versace_(song))
- **Spark:** A mixtape cut that moved fast enough to make the biggest rapper alive change his flow within a week of hearing it.
- **Billboard footnote:** peaked **#99 Hot 100** — the definitive demonstration that chart peak is uncorrelated with cultural detonation in this lane. **Use this song to justify the playlist's thesis.**

## Q4: Bay Area / West Coast street records

### Takeaway
The Bay's 2013 detonations were **dance-driven and Vine/YouTube-native**, and "Gas Pedal" is the only record in this entire report with a genuine week-by-week first-month view curve on the record. That curve (~100K week 1 → 600K by ~2 months) is notably *small* by later standards and shows the record was a **build**, not an instant explosion — useful calibration data.

### Cited Findings

**Sage the Gemini — "Gas Pedal", 2013 — IGNITION 3 — the only song here with a real first-month curve**
- **Video premiered March 21, 2013** — HARD — [IMVDb](https://imvdb.com/video/sage-the-gemini/gas-pedal)
- **Week-by-week (the best velocity data in this report):** "received **100,000+ YouTube views in its first week**, then **300,000+ in its second week**, to **400,000+ views in a month**, and was currently at **600,000+** " — HARD, from a piece dated **May 28, 2013** — [The Source, May 28, 2013](https://thesource.com/2013/05/28/sage-the-gemini-has-the-internet-goin-nuts-with-new-video-gas-pedal/)
- Dance origin: the Gas Pedal dance "started with a guy named Chonkie, from the Oakland dance crew The Turf Feinz"; "countless fans uploaded their own takes to both YouTube and Vine, making the impact simply seismic" — VIBE with named origin — [BuzzFeed](https://www.buzzfeed.com/azafar/sage-the-gemini-bay-area-interview)
- **Spark:** An Oakland turf-dance crew's move got attached to a slow-rolling beat and fans re-uploaded themselves doing it until the song was inescapable.
- **CALIBRATION WARNING for the synthesizer:** by the numbers this is a *modest* ignition — 100K first week. It became huge, but slowly. If the playlist is strictly ignition-velocity, "Gas Pedal" scores ~3, not 5, and this is the one case where we can prove it rather than guess.
- **Billboard footnote:** Hot 100 #29 (not verified here); "Red Nose" peaked #52 Hot 100, **#10 Hot Rap** — HARD — [Wikipedia: Red Nose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Nose)

**Sage the Gemini — "Red Nose", 2013 — IGNITION 3**
- Released **March 19, 2013** as his debut single — HARD — [Wikipedia: Red Nose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Nose); [Songfacts](https://www.songfacts.com/facts/sage-the-gemini/red-nose)
- Same dance-upload mechanism as "Gas Pedal" — the two "resurrected dance in the Bay" — VIBE — [BuzzFeed](https://www.buzzfeed.com/azafar/sage-the-gemini-bay-area-interview)
- **Billboard footnote:** #52 Hot 100, #14 Hot R&B/Hip-Hop, #10 Hot Rap.

**Nef the Pharaoh — "Big Tymin'", 2015 — IGNITION 3 (regional detonation, national slow-burn)**
- 2015 breakout single; **over 50 million YouTube views** (cumulative, undated) and **#1 on regional Bay Area charts**, driven by hyphy dance challenges — HARD-ish cumulative / VIBE mechanism — [The Artist Connect](https://theartistconnect.org/vallejo-legend-nef-the-pharaoh-from-big-tymin-to-changszn-3-a-bay-icon-still-evolving/); [COYOTE Media](https://www.coyotemedia.org/nef-pharaoh-underrated-rapper-of-his-generation-is-hiding-in-plain-sight/)

## Q5: Premiere-channel landscape and platform mechanics (cross-cutting)

### Takeaway
Between 2012 and 2016 the ignition platform shifted three times — **director-owned YouTube channels (2012 Chicago) → Vine 6-second loops (2014 Brooklyn/ATL) → SoundCloud + a single superstar sample/tweet (2015–16)** — and each shift left a different (and progressively better-documented) data trail.

### Cited Findings
- **2012, director channels:** DGainz's personal YouTube channel, not a label or WSHH channel, hosted the 30M-view "I Don't Like" — HARD — [Wikipedia: I Don't Like](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Don%27t_Like); DGainz's own account of the era — [BET interview with DGainz](https://www.bet.com/article/qo2tu6/i-don-t-like-director-dgainz-talks-drill-music)
- **2012, WSHH as distribution:** "The song came by way of WorldStarHipHop… young hip-hop consumers were more apt to run to WorldStarHipHop than 106 & Park" — VIBE — [Complex](https://www.complex.com/music/a/andre-gee/chief-keef-i-dont-like-10-year-anniversary)
- **2014, Vine:** a single named user (Vladimir Francois) looping six seconds is the documented ignition event for "Hot Nigga" — HARD — [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/how-bobby-shmurda-hot-boy-came-to-life/); the same mechanism is credited for "U Guessed It" — [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/bitch-you-guessed-ityou-was-right)
- **2015–16, SoundCloud + superstar sample:** "Panda" (SoundCloud, Dec 15, 2015) → Kanye's *TLOP* sample (Feb 2016) → #1 Hot 100 (May 7, 2016) — HARD chain — [Wikipedia: Panda (song)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panda_(song)); [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/desiigner-panda-billboard-hot-100-number-1/)
- **2014, Drake-as-platform:** Drake's tweet of the "Tuesday" remix (Aug 12, 2014) is described as the archetype of "Drake-stimulus" overnight profile elevation — VIBE — [Complex](https://www.complex.com/music/a/matthew-ritchie/ilovemakonnen-tuesday-drake-remix-interview)
- **DatPiff:** confirmed as a release channel for *Back from the Dead* (March 12, 2012, DatPiff + iTunes) — HARD date, **no download numbers obtainable** — [Wikipedia: Back from the Dead (mixtape)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back_from_the_Dead_(mixtape))

### Inferences
- **Lyrical Lemonade / Cole Bennett and No Jumper are essentially absent from the 2012–2016 window in my findings** — Bennett's detonation era begins ~2017–18 and belongs to whichever agent covers 2017–2024. I found no evidence of either as a premiere channel for the records in this lane.
- For records where view data is unrecoverable, the synthesizer can rank on three archive-free proxies: (1) **days from organic circulation to official single release**, (2) **days from drop to a major-artist remix/co-sign**, (3) **days from drop to a major-label deal**. All three are documented for the top-tier records here.

### Gaps
- Could not verify any "Raingutter" or "Bank Roll" channel premiere data — I found no sources on these channels at all.
- No WSHH view counts of any kind.
- No LiveMixtapes or DatPiff download totals of any kind.

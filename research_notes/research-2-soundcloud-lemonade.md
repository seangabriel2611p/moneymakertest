# SoundCloud Rap & the Cole Bennett / Lyrical Lemonade Machine, 2015–2019: Ignition-Velocity Candidates

**Researcher note / methodology caveat (read first):** All research was online-only, conducted 2026-09-19. **WebFetch was blocked by the environment's network policy for every external domain** (403 on CONNECT; confirmed on `en.wikipedia.org`, `billboard.com`, `www.complex.com`, `www.rollingstone.com`, `pennyfractions.ghost.io`, `kids.kiddle.co`, and then confirmed environment-wide by the coordinator). **Every figure below was therefore obtained through WebSearch result extraction rather than by reading the source page directly.** The URL and date cited are the ones surfaced in the search result. Treat every figure as **needing one spot-check** before publication, especially the ones tagged with a low-confidence source. Where a number's *as-of date* was not recoverable, I say so explicitly rather than guessing.

**Tagging convention:** `HARD` = a specific, dated, countable figure (views, plays, streams, dollars, chart dates). `VIBE` = retrospective punditry, narrative claim, or an undated "currently has X views" figure that tells you nothing about *velocity*.

**Ignition benchmark calibration (all = 5):** "I Don't Like," "Hot Nigga," "Panda," "Magnolia," "Lucid Dreams," "Shotta Flow," "Ransom."

**Count:** 38 fully-worked song candidates with sourced signals (Sections 1–2), 5 spark events (Section 3), and ~30 additional in-lane titles with no verified numbers (Section 4).

## Section 1 — Tier 5 / Tier 4-5 Candidates (strongest ignition evidence)

### 1. "Lucid Dreams" — Juice WRLD (2017 SoundCloud / 2018 single) — **Ignition 5**
- **Premiere channel:** SoundCloud, 15 June 2017, as "Lucid Dreams (Forget Me)" on the *999* EP; re-released as a single 4 May 2018; **Lyrical Lemonade / Cole Bennett video uploaded 11 May 2018**. — [search-retrieved Wikipedia/Fandom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucid_Dreams_(Juice_Wrld_song)) (accessed 2026-09-19) — **HARD** (dates)
- **Label-velocity signal:** Interscope signed Juice WRLD in **March 2018 for $3 million** (joint venture) — at which point "Lucid Dreams" and "All Girls Are the Same" had each "broken seven figures in streams" on SoundCloud, and **he had performed in public exactly once**, at a party for classmates at Homewood-Flossmoor High School. — [Billboard cover story, 2019](https://www.billboard.com/music/features/juice-wrld-billboard-cover-story-2019-8501291/); [Billboard deal story](https://www.billboard.com/pro/juice-wrld-signs-interscope-3-million-joint-venture/) (accessed 2026-09-19) — **HARD** (dollar figure + date + the "one show" detail)
- **Same-week context:** the week Interscope signed Juice WRLD, **Warner Bros. signed Lil Pump for $8 million**. — [Billboard cover story](https://www.billboard.com/music/features/juice-wrld-billboard-cover-story-2019-8501291/) — **HARD**
- **Terminal scale:** Lyrical Lemonade video crossed **1 billion YouTube views on 7 August 2024**, ~6 years after upload. — [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/juice-wrld-video-lucid-dreams-1-billion-views-youtube-1235748517/); [XXL](https://www.xxlmag.com/juice-wrld-lucid-dreams-video-1-billion-views/) (accessed 2026-09-19) — **HARD but not a velocity number** (it's a 6-year cumulative)
- **Spark:** A Chicago-suburb teenager who had played one show turned a SoundCloud loosie into a $3M Interscope deal inside nine months, and Cole Bennett's video became the Lyrical Lemonade era's defining artifact.
- **Billboard footnote:** peaked No. 2 Hot 100 (secondary).
- **Flags:** none. First wave was SoundCloud/emo-rap internet, not radio.
- **Gap:** I could NOT verify first-24h or first-week view counts for the 11 May 2018 Lyrical Lemonade video, despite targeted searching. See Known Unknowns.

### 2. "Shotta Flow" — NLE Choppa (2019) — **Ignition 5**
- **Premiere channel:** self-released January 2019, YouTube video (Memphis, prop guns outside a house). — [Complex](https://www.complex.com/music/a/j-mckinney/nle-choppa-everything-you-need-to-know) (accessed 2026-09-19) — **HARD** (date)
- **Velocity — FIRST WEEK:** the video **amassed 915,000 views within the first week of release**, then **reached 10 million views in a month.** — [search-retrieved Wikipedia, "Shotta Flow"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shotta_Flow) (accessed 2026-09-19) — **HARD** — this is the cleanest week-1→month-1 curve in the entire dossier (0.9M → 10M, an ~11x month-over-week acceleration).
- **Sequel velocity:** **"Shotta Flow 2"** (February 2019) did **20 million views within two months.** — [search-retrieved Wikipedia, "Shotta Flow 2"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shotta_Flow_2) (accessed 2026-09-19) — **HARD**

- **Outcome:** he **turned down** the $3M major offers for a distribution partnership with Steve Stoute's UnitedMasters. — [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/nle-choppa-turned-down-a-3-million-record-deal-partner-united-masters/) (accessed 2026-09-19) — **HARD**
- **Spark:** A 16-year-old from Memphis went from a phone-shot yard video to a three-way major-label bidding war in under 30 days — and said no.
- **Billboard footnote:** peaked No. 36 Hot 100; RIAA Platinum.
- **Flags:** none — textbook community-first ignition.
